**This node's library, and it is public.** A library keeps knowledge somebody gave it, enumerates
what it has, and lends it out — and the lending needs nothing from the library at all. There is no
card, no front desk, and nothing here has to be up for anything here to work.

This is the **civic** one. A personal node keeps a library too and keeps it private; this node is a
public body, so its library is the public record of what it holds and what it puts forward. That is
one line of configuration — [`library.yml`](library.yml) — and not a different kind of software.

> **You are reading a README, on purpose.** This page is `library/README.md`. There is no separate
> landing page and you were not redirected to get here. The reasoning is in
> [`EXHIBIT.md`](https://github.com/FCCN-ANTIBODY/library.anecdote.channel/blob/main/EXHIBIT.md):
> an index is where a project puts what it wants you to think, and a README is where it puts what
> you need to know.

## What you can do with it, and what each costs

Three relationships, and they are not degrees of one thing. The referee for all three is
[`RESIDENCY.md`](https://github.com/FCCN-ANTIBODY/library.anecdote.channel/blob/main/RESIDENCY.md).

| | what it means | what it costs you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **holding** | this library points at your repository. The content stays inert and nobody here reads it | nothing, and it is reversible |
| **contributing** | you file something and it becomes the library's | the filing, and it is one-way |
| **residency** | you live here and keep working; you get a wing named by your mount | the wing's terms, and you may leave |

If you have arrived with a working project and no idea what any of these words mean, the door is
[**ADOPTING.md**](https://github.com/FCCN-ANTIBODY/library.anecdote.channel/blob/main/ADOPTING.md).
It is written for exactly that, and it routes rather than restating.

## The shelves

Two prefixes that are a library's own working space. **Neither is a category** — they are declared
in [`SHELVES.md`](https://github.com/FCCN-ANTIBODY/library.anecdote.channel/blob/main/SHELVES.md)
and reserved against collision in `CATEGORIES.md`.

| shelf | what goes there | here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`share/`](share/FCCN-ANTIBODY/) | held in order to be handed on — the moniker groups, the references this node publishes | [`share/FCCN-ANTIBODY/`](share/FCCN-ANTIBODY/) |
| `build/` | intermediates this library made and sources deterministically, so a lost network does not cost an artifact | empty, and advertised rather than hidden |

## The words this library reserves

The reserved category words, and what this node would put under each. **A word with nothing under
it is correct and not a gap** — reserving costs no directory, and an empty folder teaches a reader
nothing that this table does not.

| word | means | this node |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `city` | the civic node, and what a jurisdiction publishes about itself | the obvious one. Crawl recipes for a city's own publishing, and the shape of rolling documents |
| `voices` | people writing | the citation journals this node already mounts |
| `media` | broadcast | nothing yet |
| `trade` | business, money or not | nothing yet |
| `bottles` | the crate you send something in | nothing yet |
| `library` | knowledge | a library holding another library's catalogue is redundancy, not conflict |
| `land` | shapes, and who claims them | **proposed, not canon.** Named so nobody takes the word locally |

## What is actually here today

Plainly, because a catalogue that overstates itself has failed at the one thing it is for:

- **The engine is mounted and nothing is configured.** `.library-engine` at this root is the
  residency claim; the claim is a menu and not a startup script.
- **`share/FCCN-ANTIBODY/` enumerates. It does not yet hold.** The distinction is the whole of
  [`BOTTLES.md`](https://github.com/FCCN-ANTIBODY/library.anecdote.channel/blob/main/BOTTLES.md):
  a library that only points at things is a **catalogue**, and the claim custody is for — *this
  library has it, and has had it since then* — needs the bytes.
- **No bottles, no pile, no atlas registration.** Honest defaults fire nothing.

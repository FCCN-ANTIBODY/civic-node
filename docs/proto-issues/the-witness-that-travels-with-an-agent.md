# The witness — an advocate that goes out with a persona and keeps the notes

**Labels:** advocates, anecdote, trade, library
**Status:** proto-issue — drain by doing the work it describes, then delete this file.

Milestone: none. Raised by the operator 2026-09-17 while working out what it means for a hired
persona to *learn*. Couples to [`hire-the-engine-that-takes-an-ask.md`](hire-the-engine-that-takes-an-ask.md)
and [`dossiers-the-agentic-kind.md`](dossiers-the-agentic-kind.md). **Nothing is built.**

## The role

> *"An advocate might be **openly attached to a persona** when it goes out somewhere and observing
> not only the environment, but helping to journal for that agent… **Protective** is one word. It's
> not the best one. Imagine that it's the **responsible notetaker, not authoritative** in the session
> that the agent is being used. But certainly **another witness. And witness is the point there.**"*

Four properties, and each one is a refusal of something easier to build:

| it is | it is not |
| --- | --- |
| attached to **one** party — the persona | a neutral observer of the session |
| **openly** present | covert instrumentation |
| a notetaker | an authority over what happens |
| a **witness** | a participant, a judge, or a moderator |

**It could be no more sophisticated than the advocate engine already is** — observational, making
notes about what is going on, keeping its work on a branch of the thing it serves. This is that
pattern pointed outward at an engagement rather than inward at a repository.

### The role exists in the world, which is worth knowing before designing it

*Protective but not authoritative* is not a novel combination. It is an **appropriate adult** at an
interview, or a union representative at a disciplinary: present throughout, unmistakably on one
person's side, holding no power over the proceedings, and **taking notes.**

Three known properties transfer, and the third is the one to design around:

1. Their partisanship is declared rather than hidden, which is what makes it acceptable.
2. They do not interrupt, and their not-interrupting is not consent.
3. **Their presence changes the room by existing.** A party who knows there is a record behaves
   differently.

That third one is not a defect to engineer away — **it is most of the value**, and it is also the
argument that settles covert versus open. A covert witness gives up the behavioural effect entirely
and buys nothing but the ability to surprise someone with a transcript.

> **A witness that is not announced is not this thing.** Openly attached, or absent.

## What it writes down, which is narrower than it sounds

The operator drew the line precisely, and it is the most useful constraint in this document:

> *"If there are fifteen rounds of work and the first twelve are the same thing and the person
> hiring them is yelling at it a lot — this isn't a psychology class. **What I don't need is an
> articulation of insults. What I do need is understanding what was not helpful.**"*

So the record is **of non-progress, not of affect.**

And the good news is that non-progress is the *easier* thing to observe. Twelve rounds circling one
unmet request is visible structurally — the same need restated, the work not moving — and detecting
it **requires no model of anybody's emotional state.** The yelling is not the datum. The repetition
is. The yelling is only how a human noticed the repetition first.

Two things follow:

- **A privacy dividend, for free.** *"Rounds 3–14 restated the same unmet need; the register the
  dossier supplies was not the thing missing"* carries the whole usable signal and transcribes
  nobody's frustration. That is the operator's *not a psychology class* honoured mechanically rather
  than by good manners.
- **It is legible to the hirer too.** A record of what did not land is something a hirer can read
  without being accused of anything, which matters if the witness is announced.

## Why this closes a loop the market cannot

[`hire`](hire-the-engine-that-takes-an-ask.md) accepts the market as the measurement, and records the
one thing that concedes:

> *It measures the wrong unit for the author. The market says which **harnesses** are worth hiring.
> It never says which **fragment inside one** was dead weight.*

**The witness journal is the missing instrument.** A hire record is revealed preference over a whole
harness; a witness record is evidence about *what failed to help inside an engagement*. Those are the
two halves, and the second one is the half an author can act on.

It is also better than the cold-start substitute the dossier document proposes. An ablation asks
whether a fragment changes an output; a witness saw whether the output helped.

## Learning, and the line it must not cross

> *"I kind of expect my personas… to learn, I'll say."*

The hazard is immediate and the dossier document already ruled against it in another guise: **the
engine may help you structure a dossier; it may not help you fill one.** A persona that learns by
quietly editing its own fragments reproduces exactly the failure that made every character on those
platforms sound the same — generated text in an authored slot, with no way to tell which is which.

So:

> **The journal accumulates. The dossier stays authored.**

A witness may say *this fragment did not help, eleven times*. It may not rewrite the fragment. The
edit remains a person's act, made with the evidence in hand — which keeps provenance intact, keeps
`CONSTITUTION` signable, and keeps *core ingredients* from decaying into empty calories one automatic
improvement at a time.

## Where it lives

The advocate pattern is already *"autonomous but collaborative… it implements itself by keeping its
work on a branch of the repository it serves,"* and *"many branches means many advocates."*

A persona going out to work is a concern, so it gets a branch. Which means a wisp that has been
hired eleven times has **eleven engagement branches on its own repository** — and that is the
`standalone repository holding just the thing` from the lifecycle earning its keep a second time: the
witness has somewhere to write that travels with the wisp and not with the hirer.

## Not decided here

- **Whose journal is it.** The persona's, the compiler's, or the hirer's — they will want different
  things, and the answer probably differs by hiring mode. **Hired local** means the node observed it
  and can hold it; **hired away is just a citation**, so there is no node in the room and possibly no
  witness at all. That asymmetry is the same one that biases the work history, and it is unresolved
  in both places.
- **Whether the hirer consents or is merely told.** *Openly attached* settles disclosure and not
  permission. This is the **third** consent axis this project has turned up — after the subject's
  (may a dossier about me exist) and the hirer's (may the node retain what my order produced) — and
  three unwritten consent records is now the largest hole in the design.
- **What happens to a journal when an engagement ends**, and whether a wisp carries its full history
  to the next hirer or arrives clean. Both are defensible and they are very different products.
- **Whether `witness` is the word.** It is the operator's and it is close. `second` — in the sense a
  duel has one: attached to one party, present throughout, does not fight — fits the *protective but
  not authoritative* shape almost exactly, but collides with *"you are the second factor"* already in
  `anecdote.channel/AGENTS.md`. `chaperone` and `steward` both smuggle in authority the role refuses.
